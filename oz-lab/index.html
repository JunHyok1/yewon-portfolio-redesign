Overview

Midwest universities lack a startup ecosystem for students, so four of us, including me, founded OZ Lab, based on the idea of an Opportunity Zone. We grew from 4 founding members to 35 by interviewing applicants: about 40 applied and 26 were accepted.

Key decisions

  1. Contacted LIKELION US right after founding.
    Why
    We needed free coding lessons and a national network we could not build alone.
    Result
    OZ Lab became the LIKELION US Purdue Chapter.
  2. Turned Seminar 2 into a portfolio workshop.
    Why
    Feedback from Seminar 1 showed that freshmen and sophomores needed practical career preparation.
    Result
    Seminar 2, “How to Create a Portfolio”, was designed for them.
  3. Moved team files from Google Drive to a Microsoft Teams workspace hosted by our faculty advisor, with one channel per team.
    Why
    Google Drive had become hard to manage and to secure, and files were lost when officers changed.
    Result
    Files are organized per team and stay in place across officer turnover, and I follow all six team chats as President.

Results

1st place at the InnovateHer Hackathon (Jan 2025), 3rd place at the BoilerMake Hackathon, 1st place at the LIKELION US Ideation competition, and several team projects reaching MVP stage.
