Chatit

Founding Member & iOS Developer · Apr – Dec 2025

A campus timetable and community app, built by a three-person founding team.

  • 300+ users
  • App Store, Oct 2025

Problem

There was no campus-specific app for class timetables or class discussion, so students were saving their class schedules as phone screenshots.

Research

We found this problem through student interviews.

Solution

A campus timetable and community app, built with Flutter and Firebase. I led the core timetable feature.

Impact

Launched on the App Store in Oct 2025 and reached 300+ users through in-person outreach at campus libraries.

Reflection

Growth plateaued after launch. Students were already on established channels like Reddit and Snapchat, and the cost of switching was high.

Tools

Flutter · Firebase · iOS
