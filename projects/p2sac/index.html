Overview

Marketing consulting for the Purdue Process Safety & Assurance Center (P2SAC): a LinkedIn content analysis and the brochure for the 2026 Spring P2SAC Conference (May 5–7, 2026).

Users

P2SAC, the client, and the audience it reaches on LinkedIn.

My role

Analyzed the account's content, presented a strategy to faculty and the client, and executed it, writing the copy and creating the visuals.

Key decision

Benchmarked 38 posts on impressions, CTR, and reach ratio across four content types before recommending any changes.

Key finding

Event recaps had the highest click-through rate (14.88%, 5 posts), ahead of event promotion posts.

Outcome

Presented the strategy to faculty and the client, then executed it across 22 posts.

Brochure

Designed and produced for the 2026 Spring P2SAC Conference, including layout, printing and on-site distribution.
